## Behavior and ownership

The closed tool schema accepts `/attachments/{canonical-lowercase-UUID}` with the existing output displayName and optional contentType. Directory/filename aliases, traversal, zero/noncanonical IDs, pending and foreign sources do not authorize export. The actual claimed Run/tenant/Session resolves a bound original and its entry, canonical original name/type/size/checksum and private observation hash. Source capture verifies raw immutable bytes; it does not parse Office files or materialize a Workspace copy.

Current executor Session and exact child Attachment reads remain independent of creation authority, the original human and recipient downloads. No home/workspace permission or optional reread-tool capability is required by the shared export rule. Raw missing source reads cannot become a creation grant. Source/creation policy may require Review; current qualified humans need the compound source and approval scopes. Filtering applies before counts/pages. Revocation preserves eligible cancellation and prevents delivery/publication under former authority.

`PreparedArtifactSource` and the incumbent source observation append nullable AttachmentId/EntryId/AttachmentName with null omission, preserving prior inline/workspace/resource action shapes. The exact action binds the actual original; semantic root correlation retains the existing source intent. The storage-only Attachment source facet freezes verified originals and repeats native checks inside the existing effect boundary without reentering IAM or another database transaction. Both atomic store owners also recheck actual bound ownership/entry/metadata. No new tool, Runtime, approval engine, blob index, receipt owner or public materialize route is introduced.

Migration `20261019132944_TenantAttachmentArtifactSource` extends the incumbent approval CHECK with the closed Attachment family, canonical source/entry IDs, original name, exact path, immutable byte/hash bounds and exclusion of resource/other operation payloads. Unsafe downgrade with these rows refuses atomically. Installed databases/data were not reset or migrated.
