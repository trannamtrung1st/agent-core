# Product

Tooling context for Impeccable and UI work. `/docs` is authoritative.

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyday members talk to the General Assistant or another permitted agent. Advanced users follow work, files and approvals. Creators configure reusable Definitions and independent Instances. Reviewers inspect evidence. Organization administrators manage resources/access; platform operators use a separate System scope.

## Product Purpose

Agent Core is an organization workspace for persistent agents, natural conversation and authorized work with inspectable results. Conversational presence remains foundational: continuity, interruption, replaceable STT/LLM/TTS and one Session for text and voice. Current capabilities include Spaces/Environments, rich files, bounded Tasks, Goals, Formal Workflows, evaluations, automation, protected models/connections, IAM and platform operations.

Read the [Product Experience Contract](../../docs/product-experience-contract.md), [Vision](../../docs/01-product-vision.md) and [Frontend](../../docs/13-frontend-implementation-spec.md).

## Positioning

Conversation is the ordinary entry. Agent building and administration are contextual. Configuration, execution and verification retain independent owners. Instructions and evaluation success never grant operation authority.

## Operating Context

React/Vite/strict TypeScript/Zustand and direct Ant Design v6. Same-origin HTTP and SignalR/MessagePack. Native .NET + Vite with disposable Synthetic fixtures is the key-free development path. Real providers, physical voice devices, native sandbox and deployment require separate qualification.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Explicit sign-in/invitation and organization choice. Ordinary Open enters Chat; Manage enters agent administration. Work/Build/Connect/Organization group existing destinations. Agent/draft navigation is contextual; Account/System use a scope menu. No guessed-tenant fallback. Chat Tools preserves separate Workflow/Task/Goal/file/sandbox/attention owners and stable focus return. Settings retains sparse inheritance, revision witnesses and recoverable drafts. Protected previews remain source-owned and sanitized. Credentials/secrets remain protected. Group Chat, marketplaces, public no-login flows, new integrations and unrestricted autonomy remain deferred. No second UI kit or replacement state/router framework.

## Brand Commitments

Agent Core. Quiet dark evergreen surfaces, warm white text, readable mint links and explicit scope context. Native Ant Design controls, honest Synthetic labels and attributable outcomes. No invented claims, proof or successful effects.

## Evidence on Hand

[Order 6 ledger](../../docs/reports/product-experience-delivery-2026-10-30.md) and the coverage manifest separate implementation from qualification. Historical evidence retains its original source/profile limits and does not accept the redesign.

## Product Principles

Conversation first. Context before configuration. Independent human/agent authority. Service-owned audio/transport. One Session. Clear labels and recovery. No feature loss. Actual interaction and visually inspected browser evidence before acceptance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Named controls, meaningful headings, visible focus, keyboard operation and restoration, color-independent statuses, reduced-motion feedback. Verify 1440×900, 768×1024, 390×844, 320px stress and intermediate layouts. Synthetic browser checks do not imply blanket WCAG, physical-device or provider certification.
